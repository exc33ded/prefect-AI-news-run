Building production-grade AI systems with five layers of engineering

A widely shared engineering breakdown argues that production AI systems now require five layers of engineering rather than prompt engineering alone: prompt, harness, loop, graph, and the surrounding execution environment. Harness engineering covers tools, orchestration, permissions, guardrails, observability, and verification; loop engineering covers iterative plan-execute-reflect-retry workflows that stop only when success criteria are met; and graph engineering covers coordination between multiple specialized agents. The framing matters because it explains why teams that only tune prompts plateau, while those that build the execution and verification scaffolding get reliable agent behavior. It also maps these layers to popular frameworks and platforms, making it a practical checklist for agent architecture.
