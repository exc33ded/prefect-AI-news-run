Prompting & Agentic Workflow Techniques 5 stories
Compound Engineering vs Loop Engineering | AI Builder Club

This analysis distinguishes compound engineering from loop engineering for coding agents. Compound engineering, coined at Every by Kieran Klaassen, runs plan-work-review-compound cycles that write lessons into files for the next session, while loop engineering designs recurring jobs with a trigger, state, stop condition, and executable verification. Will Larson reportedly called the former a cheap, useful experiment implementable in an hour, and the phrase drew about 3,600 U.S. Google searches a month in June 2026.
