Point-in-Time Adaptation for Financial Language Models

The paper “Point-in-Time Adaptation for Financial Language Models” tackles the pervasive look‑ahead bias that occurs when language models are trained on financial texts published after the period they are evaluated on. The authors propose a point‑in‑time adaptation technique that fine‑tunes models only on data available up to the backtest date, thereby aligning the training distribution with the test period. Experiments on standard financial backtesting datasets show that the bias is reduced by several percentage points, leading to more trustworthy performance estimates. This work matters because it offers a practical solution for deploying language models in regulated finance environments where data leakage can have costly consequences.
